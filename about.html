About the project

A map of every UK shop selling Pokémon cards

Free to use, free of ads, and kept current by the people who actually visit these shops.

  • 🗺️

    UK-wide coverage

    Local game stores, hobby shops and the bigger retailers, all on one map.

  • 🆓

    Always free

  • 🤝

    Community-run

    Anyone can submit a shop, so the map improves every time someone finds one.

Hey all,

I've put together a map of shops around the UK that sell Pokémon cards. It covers everything from local game stores and hobby shops to the bigger retailers.

I know there are other versions of this kind of map floating around, but I wanted to make one that will always be free and kept up to date by the community. Everything you need is right here on this site — the interactive map and the store submission system.

If you know of a shop that isn't listed yet, you can submit it directly through the site. This way, collectors and players can find new places to pick up packs, singles, or accessories — and maybe even discover some local game shops they didn't know about.

Thanks for any contributions, and happy hunting! 🙌

Spotted a shop that's missing?
